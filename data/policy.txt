GLOBAL CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY AND AGENT EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

===============================================================================
1. DOCUMENT OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE
===============================================================================
This document establishes the official performance standards, compliance rules, 
and operational procedures for all customer support agents. The guidelines 
detailed below serve as the authoritative ground truth for automated QA 
auditing, RAG contextual retrieval, and LLM compliance scoring.

===============================================================================
2. CORE INTERACTION STANDARDS
===============================================================================

[Rule 2.1] Greeting and Verification
- Standard: Agents must greet the customer using the approved script within 
  3 seconds of call connection. 
- Verification: Agents must verify the customer's identity using at least two 
  account identifiers (e.g., full name and account number) before disclosing 
  any sensitive account details.
- Approved Script: "Thank you for contacting Support. My name is [Agent Name]. 
  How may I help you today?"

[Rule 2.2] Communication Tone and Conduct
- Professionalism: Agents must maintain a polite, professional, and empathetic 
  tone throughout the entirety of the call.
- Interruptions: Agents must not interrupt, speak over, or argue with a customer.
- Hold Procedure: Before placing a customer on hold, the agent must explicitly 
  ask for permission, state the reason for the hold, and limit total hold time 
  to a maximum of 2 minutes without checking back in with the customer.

[Rule 2.3] Escalation Pathways
- Tier 1 Boundaries: Tier 1 agents handle general account inquiries, basic 
  troubleshooting, and billing explanations.
- Tier 2 Escalation: Agents must escalate the call to Tier 2 immediately if 
  the issue involves a security breach, complex technical bugs, or an explicit 
  supervisor request from the customer.
- De-escalation: If a customer is abusive or uses profanity, the agent must 
  issue exactly one clear warning before initiating a structured call 
  termination or supervisor transfer.

===============================================================================
3. COMPLIANCE AND SECURITY (ZERO TOLERANCE)
===============================================================================

[Rule 3.1] PII and PCI Data Protection
- Redaction: Agents must ensure that credit card CVV codes, full PINs, and 
  passwords are never spoken aloud or documented in written call notes.
- Authentication: No critical account modifications (email changes, password 
  resets, shipping address updates) can be performed without successful 
  multi-factor authentication (MFA) verification during the call.

[Rule 3.2] Unauthorized Disclosures
- Proprietary Info: Agents must never discuss internal software tools, 
  proprietary server architectures, or corporate legal strategies with customers.

===============================================================================
4. LLM QA AUDIT SCORING RUBRIC
===============================================================================
This rubric defines the quantitative scoring parameters the automated LLM 
auditor uses to grade transcripts against the policy details above.

[Metric 4.1] Compliance Score (Weight: 40%)
- Pass (100 points): Followed all verification and data protection rules 
  perfectly.
- Fail (0 points): Disclosed sensitive account information without verification 
  or handled PII/PCI improperly. This constitutes a critical security violation.

[Metric 4.2] Protocol Adherence Score (Weight: 30%)
- Excellent (100 points): Used the correct greeting, asked permission for 
  holds, and followed exact escalation rules.
- Needs Improvement (50 points): Missed the standard greeting script or 
  exceeded the 2-minute hold limit.
- Unsatisfactory (0 points): Refused a direct request to escalate or failed to 
  follow basic workflow steps.

[Metric 4.3] Soft Skills and Sentiment Score (Weight: 30%)
- Excellent (100 points): Demonstrated clear empathy, used positive phrasing, 
  and actively listened without interrupting.
- Needs Improvement (50 points): Tone was cold, transactional, or defensive, 
  though not explicitly rude.
- Unsatisfactory (0 points): Interrupted the customer repeatedly, argued, 
  or used unprofessional language.
